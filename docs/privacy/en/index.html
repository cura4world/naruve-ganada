Privacy Policy

Naruve Studio · Effective 6 August 2026 · Revised 18 August 2026

Naruve Studio (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws of the Republic of Korea, and establishes and discloses the following privacy policy in order to protect the personal information of its users.

This policy has two layers. Basic processing is what is strictly necessary to provide the pronunciation assessment function, and applies when you use the app. Optional processing applies only where the user has given separate consent for the purpose of service improvement; if you do not consent, you can still use every function of the app exactly as before.

1. Personal information collected and how it is collected

The Company collects only the minimum information necessary to provide the service. There is no sign-up procedure, and the Company does not collect information that directly identifies a user, such as name, address, telephone number or date of birth. Users are distinguished only by an anonymous identifier generated by the app.

2. Processing of voice information

The core function of this service is pronunciation assessment, so voice information is indispensable. Voice is processed as follows.

Basic processing — all users

  • Recording starts only at the moment the user presses the button; the app does not use the microphone at any other time.
  • Recorded audio is sent, by way of the Company's server, to the external voice processing providers set out in Section 5, for the purpose of pronunciation assessment.
  • For users who have not consented to service improvement, their audio is destroyed immediately once the processing required for assessment is complete, and is not stored.
  • Assessment results (scores, error types, intonation analysis) are saved as learning records.

Optional processing — users who have consented to service improvement

  • Recorded audio, assessment results and app usage records are stored in the Company's storage.
  • The stored information is used for analysis intended to improve the accuracy of the pronunciation assessment function, and for the training of artificial intelligence models. Specifically, it is used to analyse tendencies in pronunciation errors by native language and by learning stage, to develop syllable-level assessment, and to improve sentence content.
  • Consent is obtained on the app's first-launch screen and may be withdrawn at any time in the app settings. Once withdrawn, audio and usage records from that point on are not stored, and information already stored may be deleted at the user's request under Section 7.
  • Even if you do not consent, you can use every function of the app, including pronunciation assessment, in exactly the same way.

3. Purposes of processing personal information

Basic processing

  • Providing pronunciation assessment and learning feedback
  • Managing learning progress and providing statistics
  • Providing comparison information against the group of learners sharing the same native language (in statistically processed form)
  • Managing the number of free uses and verifying eligibility for paid features
  • Identifying and fixing service errors
  • Responding to user enquiries

Optional processing (with separate consent)

  • Training and validating artificial intelligence models in order to improve the accuracy of pronunciation assessment
  • Analysing tendencies in pronunciation errors by native language and by learning stage
  • Improving the learning sentences and content

Why the retention period for service improvement purposes is set at five years — a pronunciation assessment model is not built once and finished; it is retrained and improved each time new material accumulates. Early material for native languages with few users, in particular, is difficult to obtain again as time passes. The Company does not retain information indefinitely, and users may request deletion at any time regardless of the period (Section 7).

6. Provision of personal information to third parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. The transfers abroad described in Section 5 are entrustment of processing for the purpose of the Company's provision of the service, and are not provision to third parties. An exception applies where an investigative agency makes a request through lawful procedures under applicable law.

7. Users' rights and how to exercise them

Users may exercise the following rights at any time.

  • Request access to their own personal information
  • Request correction where there is an error
  • Request deletion
  • Request suspension of processing
  • Withdraw consent to service improvement

How to exercise them

Pressing "View my identifier and request deletion" on the app's settings screen displays the user's anonymous identifier, and a deletion request including that identifier can be sent to support@naruve.app. The Company will act on the request and reply with the outcome within 10 days of receiving it. You may also make the request by email directly; in that case you must include the identifier shown in the app settings, as otherwise the Company cannot locate the information concerned.

Scope of deletion

  • On a deletion request, the Company deletes all of that user's audio, assessment results, learning records and app usage records held in its storage.
  • Where the information has already been used to train an artificial intelligence model, the model itself is not reversed. A trained model does not contain the individual recordings, and separating one particular user's material out of a model is technically impossible. The Company keeps a record of which model training a user's material was used in, and will inform the user on request.
  • Statistical material that has been anonymised so that it can no longer be linked to an individual is not subject to deletion.

Relationship with deleting the app

If you delete the app, the information stored on the device is deleted with it, but information held on the Company's servers is not deleted automatically. To delete information on the servers, please request deletion by the method above before deleting the app, or let the Company know your identifier by email. If you reinstall the app, a new anonymous identifier is created, and it is not linked to the previous identifier.

8. Personal information of children under 14

The Company does not collect the personal information of children under the age of 14. Where it is confirmed that a child under the age of 14 is using the service, the Company destroys the information concerned without delay.

9. Procedure and method of destruction

Personal information whose retention period has expired or whose purpose of processing has been achieved is destroyed without delay. Information in the form of electronic files is deleted by a method that makes recovery impossible, and where printed material exists it is shredded or incinerated. Of the information stored for service improvement purposes, that whose retention period has expired is destroyed, or is anonymised so that it can no longer be linked to an individual and then retained solely for statistical purposes.

10. Measures to ensure security

  • Limiting the number of personnel who process personal information to a minimum
  • Applying encryption to the transmission channel
  • An anonymous identifier scheme that does not collect information capable of directly identifying a user
  • Not storing the audio of users who have not consented to service improvement
  • Managing access rights to storage and retaining access logs
